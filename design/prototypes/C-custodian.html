Arc Guard is open-source software (MIT), not a bank. It's non-custodial: your money sits in a smart contract on Arc that only your two keys can open. We hold no key and can't move it. Not insured. Not approved, endorsed or run by Circle. Savings uses the Galaxy USDC vault on Morpho, run by third parties, and lending carries risk.
